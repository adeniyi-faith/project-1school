Your school administrator sees a **Get your school ready** list on the dashboard. Each line ticks itself when you finish it, and the list disappears when everything is done. Work through it in this order.

## 1. Choose your kind of school

Pick Nigerian, British, American or Montessori. Adino School Systems adds the matching classes, subjects, term names and grades. It only adds what you do not have yet and never changes what you already set up. You can find it again at **Settings > School type**.

## 2. Add your logo

Your logo appears on report cards, receipts, ID cards and your website. Colours are suggested from it.

## 3. Check classes, subjects and the current term

Classes and arms are at **Classes** and **Sections**. A subject is shared: one Mathematics can be offered by many classes. Set the school year and mark the current term at **School Setup > Terms**, because fees, results and attendance are tied to the term.

## 4. Set your fees

At **Fees > Fee structures**, give each class an amount for each fee (tuition, bus, uniform). Fees are charged each term by default. Then make invoices for a class in one go.

## 5. Add people

Add staff at **Staff**. Tick *Let them sign in* to make their login in the same step. Add pupils one at a time, from an admission, or many at once from Excel at **Students > Bulk add**. Give parents their logins at **Students > Login details**.

## 6. Make your website yours

Your website is already live with starter pages. Pick a look and your colours at **Website > Themes**.

> Everything above can be done in any order. The list is only a guide.
