Every school has a public website at `/s/<your school address>`. Manage it from the **Website** menu.

## Look and pages

- **Themes**: pick a look, then your two colours. Each theme has its own layout, not only different colours.
- **Pages**: start from a template (About, Academics, Admissions, Fees, Contact), reorder, and put pages under others for sub-menus.
- **News & events**: stories and events with their own address and Google text.

## Sections fed by your records

These update by themselves and hide themselves when there is nothing to show:

| Section | Reads from |
|---|---|
| Fees by class | Fees > Fee structures (current school year) |
| Term dates and holidays | School Setup > Terms and Holidays |
| Our team | Staff with *Show on the school website* ticked on their page |
| Upcoming events | News & events posts of kind *events* |

## Talk to visitors

- **Message form** section: messages land in **Admissions > Inquiries** with a bell notice.
- **WhatsApp**: set a number in **Website > Settings** and a green chat button appears on every page.
- **Downloads** section: list the prospectus or forms by link (https only).

## Make it safe and fast

The footer credits Adino School Systems. A privacy page and cookie notice are included for every theme. The top menu never wraps; on small laptops the phone-style menu is used instead.
