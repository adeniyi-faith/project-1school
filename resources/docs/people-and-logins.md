## One person, one record

A person who works at the school is **one record**: a staff card (name, department, salary, timetable) joined to a login (what they sign in with). Making either one makes or finds the other.

| You do this | What happens |
|---|---|
| Make a login with a staff role (principal, teacher, accountant, librarian, receptionist, driver, warden, store manager) at **Settings > Admins** | A staff card is made, or joined if one has the same email |
| Add staff and tick *Let them sign in* | The staff card and the login are made together |
| Open a staff page and press *Give login* | A login is made for that person |

Passwords are shown **once** on a dialog you can copy or print. They are never stored in readable form. Use *New password* on the staff page if someone forgets theirs.

## Class teachers and subject teachers

On the **Classes** page, press the teacher button to choose a class teacher and a teacher for each subject. Teachers then land on **My Classes**, which lists their classes, students, today's lessons and shortcuts. It is a shortcut list, not a lock: teachers can still open other classes.

## Parent and student logins

Use **Students > Login details** to make logins for a class or the whole school. Each login gets a random password, printed on a slip and in an Excel list, shown once.
