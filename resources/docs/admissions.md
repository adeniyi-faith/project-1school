## The journey

1. **Apply.** A family fills in your online form (gender and date of birth included). They get a **reference** such as `APP-2026-0001` and, if they gave an email, a receipt.
2. **Follow up and test.** Staff add follow-ups and, if you use them, entrance exams and interviews.
3. **Offer or decline.** Press *Offer a place* or *Decline*. The family is emailed, and the follow-up list records it. With no email on file, the note tells you to phone them.
4. **The family accepts.** They open the status page, enter the reference and their phone number, and press *Accept the place*. Staff get a bell notice.
5. **Enrol.** One press makes the student. Two boxes, ticked by default, also make the **parent's login** and bill **this term's fees** for the class. A welcome email is sent (it never contains the password; you hand that over).

## The family's status page

Families check progress at `/apply/<school id>/status` using only their reference and the phone number they applied with. Nothing about the child is shown if the phone does not match.

## Good to know

- There is no acceptance-fee payment and no SMS yet. Email is used where an address exists.
- Fees for a child who moves up at year end are billed from **Year-end promotion > Bill fees** once the new term is set.
